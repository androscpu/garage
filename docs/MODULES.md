## Domain Modules

### PMS
- Guests, Profiles, Preferences, Contact Methods
- Rooms, Room Types, Inventory, Rate Plans, Allotments
- Front Desk: Check-in/out, Key management (integrate with door locks)
- Folios, Charges, Taxes, Fees, Packages

### Booking
- Availability, Pricing, Restrictions (LOS, CTA/CTD)
- Channels: Direct Web, Call Center, OTA (future: channel manager API)
- Offers, Promo Codes, Group Blocks

### Housekeeping
- Room Status (Dirty/Clean/Inspected/OOO)
- Task Assignment, Schedules, Priority, SLAs
- Inspections and Issue reporting

### Payments
- Tokenization, Authorization, Capture, Refunds, Disputes
- Folio integration and AR ledgers
- Payouts and reconciliation

### Integrations
- 3CX telephony, Email/SMS, Accounting (e.g., Xero), Door locks

### Reporting
- Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, Housekeeping productivity, Payment summaries
